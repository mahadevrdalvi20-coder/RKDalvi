# Owner's guide — using your R. K. DALVI app

Open the **Admin** from the app shortcut (long-press the app icon → *Admin*) or go to
`https://rkdalvi.com/admin`. Only people you add can log in.

## First time (5 minutes)

1. Open `/admin` — you will be asked to create the **owner account** (name, email, password).
2. **Settings → Business**: upload your logo, set phone, WhatsApp number, email, address,
   working hours, Instagram / Facebook / Google Business links and the four highlight numbers
   (years of experience, projects completed…). Tap **Save settings**.
3. **Settings → About Us**: write your story, add your photo and workshop photos.
4. **Dashboard → Turn on notifications** so your phone rings when a new enquiry arrives.

## Add a completed project (under 2 minutes)

1. Tap the big **+** button.
2. Tap **Choose photos / videos** and select all the photos at once (or **Take photo**).
   They upload in the background — you can keep typing. If the internet drops, uploads
   retry by themselves; if any still fail, tap **Retry failed**.
3. Type the **title**, choose the **category**, add the location and a few lines about the work.
4. Tap **Publish**. It is now live on the website and in the app.

Tips:
- The photo marked **Cover** is shown in the portfolio. Tap ☆ on another photo to change it.
- Drag the ⠿ handle to change the order.
- Mark photos as **Before** / **After** to show the comparison slider.
- Switch on **Show client name** only if the client agreed.
- Switch on **★ Featured** to show the project in the Home page slideshow.
- **Hide** takes a project off the website without deleting it.

## Enquiries

- New enquiries appear under **Enquiries** with a gold bar, and you get a notification / email.
- Tap the green **WhatsApp** button to reply instantly with a ready message, or **Call**.
- Move the lead through **New → Contacted → Site Visit → Quotation Sent → Won / Lost**,
  and add private notes (site visit time, requirements…).
- **Export to Excel (CSV)** downloads all enquiries.

## Other settings

- **Categories / Services / Testimonials / Clients / Team** — add, edit, reorder or delete.
  Only add client names, logos and reviews with the client's permission.
- **Admins** — add a family member or manager with their own login; disable or remove them anytime.
- **My account** — change your password, turn email alerts on/off, log out of all devices.

Forgot your password? Tap **Forgot password?** on the login page and use the link sent to your email.

## Sharing your work

Every project has its own link — tap **Share this project** on the project page and send it
on WhatsApp, or put your website address on visiting cards and Instagram.
